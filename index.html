About Ganesh Holidays

Named with love. Driven with passion.

Ganesh Holidays was founded in 2026 by Vignesh Ganesan, a BE graduate from Mannargudi, Tamil Nadu — named in loving memory of his father, Ganesan. With over 5 years of driving experience across South India, Vignesh started his journey as an acting driver, earning the trust of every customer through punctuality, safety, and genuine care. Today, Ganesh Holidays operates with a dedicated car and a strong network of trusted vehicles to arrange multi-car trips for families and groups. What started with one man's passion for the road has grown into a service built on reliability, personal attention, and a promise — your journey is our commitment.
